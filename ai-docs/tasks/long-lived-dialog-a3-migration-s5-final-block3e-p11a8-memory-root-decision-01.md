## Decisions

Accept observed Stats high waters through the last validated live status, with first/last offsets and closing gaps. Actual verification::finish exposes counters/resolved, not these memory fields. Do not fabricate terminal-inclusive Stats or alter product diagnostics. This clarifies section 6 of the P11 contract: observed metric caps and measured coverage remain mandatory; final cryptographic operation counters still come from actual terminal/source evidence, not sampled Stats. Preserve code-enforced storage limits independently. No terminal memory-peak claim.

Normal shutdown needs the fresh pre-request RAM sample. For the smallest deterministic design, after the shutdown request stop launching memory queries for that incarnation, record the closing tail until actual wait, and retain existing ack/final/EOF/exit checks. This removes avoidable query-vs-expected-exit races and does not convert query failures into successful coverage. Already-started query failures, malformed output, unconfirmed query cleanup and unexpected profile exit remain failures. Error/Drop cleanup starts no query. Intentional kill always proceeds even if its pre-kill observation fails, with failed coverage retained.

For the proposed 1032-row bound, make the cadence premise executable: the first progress attempt cannot occur before start+5s; subsequent progress attempts are at least5s apart, with no catch-up attempts. The start sample must not be followed by an immediate extra progress sample. Boundaries remain at most2 per lifetime, at most36 lifetimes and at most2 simultaneous original owners. Retain 2400s run-span bound; guard/ledger may not silently accept a larger observer span. This yields <=960 progress plus72 boundary rows. Pure tests cover many sub5s restarts, exact5s boundaries and long query gaps. The caps are bookkeeping, not permission to enlarge the workload.

Each Stats candidate must be schema/identity/range/regression-validated before aggregation. One pending aggregate per owner may preserve maxima/offsets but cannot overwrite an earlier invalid/regressing candidate with a later valid one. Pending candidates stay tied to the unchanged lifetime and must be validated against the same actual owned handle checkpoint before acceptance. No samples or candidates cross restarts. Explicitly preserve observed-only interpretation in the final coverage type.
